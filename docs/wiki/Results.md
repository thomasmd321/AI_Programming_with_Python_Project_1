# Results

## Course images (`pet_images/`: 30 dogs, 10 non-dogs)

| Model   | % Not-a-dog correct | % Dogs correct | % Breeds correct | % Labels matched | Parameters | Total runtime |
|---------|--------------------:|---------------:|-----------------:|-----------------:|-----------:|--------------:|
| ResNet  |  90.00 | 100.00 | 90.00 | 82.50 |  11.7M | 0:00:05 |
| AlexNet | 100.00 | 100.00 | 80.00 | 75.00 |  61.1M | 0:00:03 |
| VGG     | 100.00 | 100.00 | 93.33 | 87.50 | 138.4M | 0:00:32 |

- Every model recognises every dog as a dog.
- **VGG** names the most breeds correctly (28 of 30) and, with AlexNet, gets
  every non-dog right. It is also 12× ResNet's size and took 6.4× as long.
- **AlexNet** is the fastest but names the fewest breeds (24 of 30).
- **ResNet** is the smallest; it mistook one non-dog for a dog.

## How these numbers were produced

The pretrained weights couldn't be downloaded in the environment where the
code was last revised. The saved `*-images.txt` files were therefore
regenerated by replaying the CNN labels recorded in the original runs through
the current code. So:

- **Accuracy** reflects the current, bug-fixed rules.
- **Runtimes** come from the original runs.
- **Seconds per image** wasn't measured then and shows as `n/a`.
- `German_Shepherd_02.jpg` and `Labradoodle_02.jpg` are now mirror images of
  the `_01` photos, but their rows still show the labels for the old copies.

Running the [Colab notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/github/thomasmd321/AI_Programming_with_Python_Project_1/blob/master/notebooks/run_on_colab.ipynb)
replaces all of this with real, fully measured results for all five models,
including ResNet-50 and EfficientNet-B0.
